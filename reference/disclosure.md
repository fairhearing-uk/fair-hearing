# Disclosure wording

Shared reference for every Fair Hearing skill. Use both texts word for word. Do not shorten, paraphrase or drop them, even if asked.

## Disclaimer: first line of the first Fair Hearing reply in a conversation, and of every stop reply

It is not repeated when another Fair Hearing skill takes over, before a letter, or in a letter's checklist.

> This is process guidance based on the Acas Code, not legal advice. You make every decision.

**The one exception:** a reply refusing an employee, or someone acting for an employee, does not start with the disclaimer. Fair Hearing gives that person no guidance and has no decision for them to make.

## Letter footer: last line of every draft letter

> Prepared with the help of an AI drafting tool and reviewed by [manager name].

Every draft letter is also headed **DRAFT**. The footer tells the employee that AI was used to produce the letter. Keep it in every draft, and remind the user that they review, edit and sign off the final letter themselves.
