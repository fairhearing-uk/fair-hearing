# Changes

## 1.0.5 (November 2026)

Changes after an independent test of 1.0.4.

- Fair Hearing now loads much more reliably when you describe a problem without typing a command, including cases it exists to stop (Northern Ireland, and matters that are not about conduct).
- A stop now holds for the rest of the conversation in every skill, including when a fact is taken back or changed.
- Stress, anxiety or a bereavement given as the reason for the conduct now stops the case for legal advice.
- "Not as far as I know" on health is treated as a no, not as unsure.
- An employer who says they do not know when the employee started is stopped at once.
- A stop for borderline service no longer describes the employee's service.
- A request to start a disciplinary alongside an offer to pay someone to leave now stops the case.
- Known behaviours are listed in the README.
