# Letter 3: outcome, written warning or final written warning

Adapted from Acas's template "Notice of written warning or final written warning" (see `ATTRIBUTION.md`). Use only after the hearing, once the user has stated their decision to give a written warning or a final written warning.

**Facts needed from the user:** the hearing date; the decision (written or final written warning); how many months the warning stays current; the nature of the misconduct; the change in conduct expected; the timescale; the likely consequence of further misconduct (their decision); who handles an appeal, how to send it (write or email, and the address) and the period for appealing (their decision).

Copy the letter below exactly. Replace each `{{NAME}}` with that clause, copied exactly from `reference/code-version.md` ("Code-dependent letter clauses"). Keep only the warning type the user decided. Fill [placeholders] only with what the user has said.

## Letter

```
DRAFT

[Today's date]

Dear [Employee name]

Notice of [written warning / final written warning]

You attended a disciplinary hearing on [date]. I am writing to inform you of your [written warning / final written warning].

{{WARNING-CONTENT}}

{{APPEAL-RIGHT}}

Yours sincerely

[Signature]
[Name of manager]

Prepared with the help of an AI drafting tool and reviewed by [manager name].
```
