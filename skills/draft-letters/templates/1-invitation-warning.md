# Letter 1: invitation to a disciplinary hearing where a warning is possible

Adapted from Acas's template "Notice of disciplinary hearing: possible warning" (see `ATTRIBUTION.md`). Use when the possible consequences the user has decided do **not** include dismissal.

**Facts needed from the user:** that they have decided there is a case to answer; the possible consequences (their decision); the hearing date, time and place; the evidence enclosed; how long before the hearing they want the companion's name; whether they have a written disciplinary procedure (and its name); the details of the alleged misconduct; the informal steps taken, or the reason none were (both may already be in the triage summary).

Copy the letter below exactly. Replace each `{{NAME}}` with that clause, copied exactly from `reference/code-version.md` ("Code-dependent letter clauses") or, for `{{COMPANION}}`, from `reference/right-to-be-accompanied.md` ("Letter clause"). Fill [placeholders] only with what the user has said.
