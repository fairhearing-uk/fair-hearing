# Letter 5: invitation to an appeal meeting

Adapted from Acas's templates "Notice of appeal meeting against warning" and "Notice of appeal meeting against a dismissal or serious disciplinary action" (see `ATTRIBUTION.md`), merged. Use once the employee has appealed.

**Facts needed from the user:** what the employee appealed against (written warning, final written warning, dismissal or other disciplinary action); the date that decision was confirmed in writing; the date of the appeal, if known; who will hear the appeal (the user's choice); the place, date and time; how long before the meeting they want the companion's name.

If the user says the appeal will be heard by someone already involved, quote the Code's appeal wording from `code-version.md` (and the size-and-resources wording if they say no one else can hear it), say the choice is theirs, and carry on.

Copy the letter below exactly. Replace `{{COMPANION}}` with the sentence copied exactly from `reference/right-to-be-accompanied.md` ("Letter clause"). Keep only the bracketed options that apply. Fill [placeholders] only with what the user has said.

## Letter

```
DRAFT

[Today's date]

Dear [Employee name]

Notice of appeal meeting

You have appealed[ on [date of appeal]] against the [written warning / final written warning / dismissal / disciplinary action] confirmed to you in writing on [date].

Your appeal will be heard by [name or role of the person hearing the appeal] at [location] on [date] at [time].

{{COMPANION}}

The decision of this appeal meeting is final and there is no further right of review.

Yours sincerely

[Signature]
[Name of manager]

Prepared with the help of an AI drafting tool and reviewed by [manager name].
```
