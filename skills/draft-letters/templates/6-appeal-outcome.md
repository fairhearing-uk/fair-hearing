# Letter 6: appeal outcome

Adapted from Acas's templates "Notice of result of appeal against a warning" and "Notice of appeal result against a dismissal or other serious disciplinary action" (see `ATTRIBUTION.md`), merged. Use only after the appeal meeting, once the user has stated the appeal decision.

**Facts needed from the user:** what was appealed against; the date of the appeal meeting; who heard it; the decision (the original decision stands, or is revoked; if revoked, whether there is no action or what the new action is: their decision); the reasons; whether they have a written disciplinary procedure (and its name).

Copy the letter below exactly. Keep only the bracketed options that apply. Fill [placeholders] only with what the user has said. If there is no written procedure, leave out "under [name of your disciplinary procedure]".

## Letter

```
DRAFT

[Today's date]

Dear [Employee name]

Notice of appeal result

You appealed against the decision of the disciplinary hearing that you be [given a written warning / given a final written warning / dismissed / subject to disciplinary action]. The appeal meeting was held on [date].

I am now writing to confirm the decision taken by [name or role of the person who heard the appeal], who conducted the appeal meeting: the decision to [the original decision] [stands / is revoked]. [If revoked: no disciplinary action is being taken / the new disciplinary action is [the new action you have decided]].

The reasons for this decision are:
[reasons].

You have now exercised your right of appeal[ under [name of your disciplinary procedure]] and this decision is final.

Yours sincerely

[Signature]
[Name of manager]

Prepared with the help of an AI drafting tool and reviewed by [manager name].
```
