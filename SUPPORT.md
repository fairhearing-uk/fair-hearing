# Support

For help with Fair Hearing, or to report a problem, email **fairhearinguk@proton.me**.

GitHub Issues are not used for this project, so please use email.

The Fair Hearing team can't advise on individual cases by email. For advice on your case, take legal advice, for example from an employment law solicitor. The Acas helpline can also explain the law and your options.

Acas helpline: 0300 123 1100, Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. The advice is free, but call charges may apply.
