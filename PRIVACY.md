# Privacy

**Fair Hearing is a set of instructions only.** It runs no code, and it calls no other service.

**It collects, stores and sends nothing itself.** Fair Hearing has no server, no database and no analytics. Its makers never see what you type.

**What you type stays in your own Claude conversation.** Anthropic handles it under its own terms and privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Your account settings there apply.

**You are responsible for your employee's personal data.** As the employer, share only what Fair Hearing needs. Initials or a job title are enough; you don't need to give names.

**Not for under-18s.** Fair Hearing is meant for adults acting for an employer.
